# Privacy - Assimilate Pro

**Last updated:** 2026-10-12

## In short

- You do not make an account.
- Your answers, stops, and scores stay on your device.
- Clearing the site data or the app data removes them.

## What is kept on your device

- The country you chose
- Who you are (only if you pick it)
- Your finished stops, quiz scores, and stories you have read
- Your language and text-size choice

This is kept in your browser or in the app. It is not sent to us.

## The website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use analytics, cookies, or advertising trackers. The "Download latest APK" button opens the release page on GitHub.

The website shows a small visit counter at the bottom of the page. To count a visit, your browser sends one request to Abacus (abacus.jasoncameron.dev), a free counter service. It adds 1 to a single total and stores nothing else: no cookies, no account, and no profile. Like any web server, Abacus may see your IP address when the request arrives. Your browser remembers, for that tab only, that the visit was counted, so reloading does not count it twice.

## The Android app

The app keeps all pages inside the app and works with no internet. The app has no visit counter. Android may check web addresses for safety (Safe Browsing).
